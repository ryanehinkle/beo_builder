How to use BEO Builder

Step 1

Batch merge each day like normal

Start with batch merging Kitchen and Manager BEOs for each day. You do not need to rename files, put them in date order, or organize them yourself.

Step 2

Upload all of the PDFs together

Click Upload BEOs and select all of the PDFs you want to combine. Again, no manual organization is needed. It will read the files and separate/organize them automatically.

Step 3

Everything is organized automatically

BEO Builder separates uploads into Manager, Kitchen, Internal, and Misc, then sorts the individual events by event date and event start time. In Printable mode, each individual event is kept on an even number of pages so the next BEO starts correctly when the final file is printed double-sided.

Step 4

Choose your merge options

Printable preserves the double-sided printing layout. Remove blank pages creates a compact version without blank pages for digital viewing. For Kitchen BEOs, you can also automatically remove flex events that contain no Kitchen items.

Step 5

Merge and download

Click Merge. The finished Manager and Kitchen PDFs are generated and downloaded automatically, they will be ordered and named with their date range.
